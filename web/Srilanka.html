About

Sri Lanka is a beautiful island nation in the Indian Ocean, located south of India. It is known for its rich culture, ancient history, tropical beaches, wildlife, tea plantations, and scenic landscapes. The country has a diverse population and a unique blend of traditions influenced by Buddhism, Hinduism, Islam, and Christianity. Sri Lanka is also famous for its Ceylon tea, spices, gemstones, and warm hospitality.

History

Lanka has a history spanning over 2,500 years. Ancient kingdoms such as Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa played an important role in developing Buddhism, irrigation, architecture, and culture. Buddhism was introduced to the island during the 3rd century BCE and became a major part of Sri Lankan society. From the 16th century, Sri Lanka was controlled by the Portuguese, Dutch, and British. Under British rule, the island was known as Ceylon. Sri Lanka gained independence from Britain on 4 February 1948 and became a republic in 1972, officially changing its name from Ceylon to Sri Lanka.

Civilization

Sri Lanka has a rich ancient civilization dating back over 2,500 years. Ancient kingdoms like Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa were known for Buddhism, advanced irrigation, agriculture, and impressive architecture. Their achievements continue to influence Sri Lankan culture and heritage today.

Languages

Sri Lanka has three main languages: Sinhala, Tamil, and English. Sinhala and Tamil are the official languages, while English is widely used in education, business, tourism, and communication.
