Riverside Sport Club

Riverside Sports Club is a friendly local club for children, teenagers, and adults who enjoy playing sports, staying active, and meeting new people. The club provides training, matches, fitness activities, and community events for members of different skill levels.

Sports Offered

  • Team Sports
    • Football
    • Basketball
    • Volleyball
  • Racquet Sports
    • Tennis
    • Badminton
    • Squash
  • Individual Sports
    • Swimming
    • Running
    • Cycling

Sports Club Terms You Should Know

Membership
A subscription that allows a person to take part in club activities and events.
Fixture
A scheduled match or event date for a particular sport.
Coach
The person responsible for training and guiding a sports team.
