About Music

Music is an art form and cultural activity whose medium is sound organized in time. General definitions of music include common elements such as pitch (which governs melody and harmony), rhythm (and its associated concepts), and the sonic qualities of timbre and texture.

History of Dance

Dance is a performing art form consisting of purposefully selected sequences of human movement. This movement has aesthetic and symbolic value, and is acknowledged as dance by performers and observers within a particular culture. Dance can be categorized and described by its choreography, by its repertoire of movements, or by its historical period or place of origin.

Music Genres

Music genres are categories that identify pieces of music as belonging to a shared tradition or set of conventions. Genres are formed by conventions that change over time as new styles emerge and are recognized. Often, works fit into multiple genres by way of borrowing and recombining these conventions.

Dance Styles

Dance styles are categorized by their unique movements, techniques, and cultural origins. Some popular dance styles include ballet, hip-hop, salsa, contemporary, jazz, and folk dances. Each style has its own history and significance within the culture it originates from.
